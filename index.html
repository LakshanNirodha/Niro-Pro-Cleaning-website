FAQ

Common Questions

Do you guarantee the bond back?+
Yes. If your real estate agent identifies any cleaning issues after our end-of-lease service, we return and re-clean at no extra charge. We have a 100% bond-back record across every single job.
Do I need to be home during the clean?+
No. Most clients leave us a key or access code. We are fully insured and background-checked, so you can have complete peace of mind while we work.
Do you supply all cleaning equipment?+
Yes, we bring everything — professional steam equipment for carpets and upholstery, scrubbing machines for tiles, all eco-friendly products, microfibre cloths and vacuums. You don't need to provide anything.
How far in advance should I book?+
For end-of-lease cleans, book at least 3-5 days in advance. For regular cleans, we can often fit you in within 24-48 hours. Call us on 0411 746 994 for urgent bookings.
Are you available on weekends?+
Yes, we work 7 days a week including most public holidays. Weekend rates may apply for some services.
How do I pay?+
We accept bank transfer, cash and card. Payment is made on or after the day of the clean. We provide a professional tax invoice for all jobs.
Do you offer commercial contracts?+
Yes. We offer regular commercial cleaning contracts for offices, gyms, retail spaces, schools and other commercial properties. After-hours service is available. Contact us for custom contract pricing.
